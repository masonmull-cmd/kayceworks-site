  1. 1 · Install

    Open Signal-Bot-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder. It installs for your Windows user only, so no admin password is needed.

    SignalBot opens on the activation screen. Paste the code from your email. It looks like SB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, and dashes or capitals don't matter.

    The setup wizard signs you in to Robinhood, finds your signal channel and sets your limits. It starts in dry run and sends nothing until you switch to Live.

Install questions

How do I update to a new version?

Download it from this page and run it. It installs over the old one, and your settings, limits and activation stay.

I can't find my activation code.

It's emailed to you right after you subscribe, from mason.mull@kayceworks.com. Search your inbox and spam folder for “SignalBot”. Still nothing? Email mason.mull@kayceworks.com from the address you subscribed with.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings → Apps, find Signal Bot and choose Uninstall. Your settings, history and activation stay on the PC, so reinstalling picks up where you left off.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Open your subscription page and sign in with the email you subscribed with. You can cancel or update your card there.
