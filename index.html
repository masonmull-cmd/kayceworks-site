Questions

Do I need to add a bot to the signal server?

No. SignalBot reads the channel in the Discord app on your own PC, so it works with any signal channel you can see.

Is it a self-bot? Will it touch my Discord account?

No. It never logs in to Discord and never uses your token. It only reads what your Discord app already shows.

Which broker does it trade with?

Robinhood, through Robinhood's official Agentic Trading. You sign in on Robinhood's own page; SignalBot never sees your password, and the login stays encrypted on your PC.

Does my PC have to stay on?

Yes. SignalBot is a Windows app; keep the PC on and Discord open on the signal channel during market hours.

Can I try it without risking money?

Yes. Every setup starts in dry run with live Robinhood prices: it shows exactly what it would buy and how fast, and sends nothing until you switch to Live.

Does it sell for me?

Only if you turn on take-profit. Otherwise you close every position yourself in Robinhood.

Is this financial advice?

No. SignalBot copies calls you choose to follow. It doesn't pick trades or promise results.

SignalBot is software that copies trade calls you choose to follow; it does not give financial advice or pick trades.
